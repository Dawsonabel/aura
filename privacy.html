This Privacy Policy explains what information Aura ("the App", "we", "us") collects, how it's used, and the choices you have. Aura is only for students 13 years of age or older; we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 13.

Information we collect

  • Phone number — used to verify your identity via a one-time SMS code at signup. We do not use your phone number for marketing.
  • Profile information — first/last name, username, gender, grade, age, school, and an optional profile photo you upload.
  • Activity data — the polls you answer, the classmates you select, friend connections, blocks, and reports you file. When another student picks you in a poll, we store that pick; the identity of who picked you is not shown to you unless you choose to reveal it (via coins or a subscription feature), and never if that person has chosen to remain anonymous.
  • Purchases — if you buy a subscription or in-app item, Apple processes the payment; we store only the transaction identifier and subscription status needed to grant your purchase, not your payment details.
  • Device/technical data — standard request metadata (IP address, timestamps) used for rate-limiting and abuse prevention.

How we use your information

  • To operate the core features of the app: polls, the "flames" inbox, friends, and school-based matching.
  • To verify you're a real person and prevent spam/abuse (rate limiting on verification codes and admin logins).
  • To respond to reports and enforce our Terms of Service, including blocking or removing accounts that violate them.
  • We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

Who can see your information

  • Other students see only what you choose to share (your name, username, photo, and picks you've made) and never your phone number.
  • School administrators using the admin dashboard can see full account details, including phone numbers, for students at their school, in order to manage rosters and respond to safety reports.
  • Service providers we use to operate the app: Twilio (SMS delivery), Neon (database hosting), and Apple (payment processing for subscriptions). Each only receives the minimum data needed to perform its function.
  • We may disclose information if required by law, or to protect the safety of a user or the public.

Your choices and rights

  • Block and report — you can block any user and report content directly in the app.
  • Delete your account — deleting your account (Settings → Delete Account) permanently removes your profile and unlinks you from other users' friend lists. This cannot be undone.
  • Access or correct your data — contact us at [SUPPORT EMAIL] to request a copy of your data or a correction.

Data retention

We retain account data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, your profile and personal identifiers are removed; anonymized or aggregate records may be retained for safety and legal-compliance purposes.

Children's privacy

Aura requires all users to be at least 13 years old and does not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If we learn that we've collected information from a user under 13, we will delete it. Parents or guardians who believe their child has provided us information despite our age requirement should contact us at [SUPPORT EMAIL].

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We'll update the "Last updated" date above when we do.
